# Community Code of Conduct

Field Runtime Core is infrastructure for consequential work. Its community should
model the clarity, accountability, and respect expected from the product.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and constructive.
- Critique ideas and implementations, not people.
- Welcome people across backgrounds, identities, abilities, and experience levels.
- Make uncertainty and relevant conflicts visible.
- Respect privacy, attribution, consent, and the synthetic-data boundary.
- Accept moderation decisions without retaliation.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized conduct or attention, deliberate
intimidation, stalking, doxing, sustained disruption, retaliation, and publication
of another person's private information without permission are not acceptable.

This policy applies in repository spaces and when someone publicly represents the
project or community.

## Reporting

Do not publish sensitive conduct details in an issue. When GitHub presents a
private advisory form for this repository, submit the report there and title it
`Community conduct report`:

<https://github.com/Mehtapozkan/fieldruntime-core/security/advisories/new>

If the form is unavailable, do not disclose incident details publicly. A public
issue may request a private reporting path without naming people or describing the
incident. GitHub's platform reporting mechanisms remain available independently.

Include what happened, where and when it occurred, available evidence, immediate
safety concerns, and the outcome requested. Do not include customer data or
unrelated private information.

## Handling and enforcement

A maintainer named in a report must not investigate or decide it. The repository
owner should appoint a conflict-free maintainer or seek a neutral reviewer when
practical. If the owner is named and no independent reviewer is available, the
project cannot promise internal adjudication; the reporter may use GitHub's
platform reporting mechanisms. Reports will be shared only as needed to
investigate, protect participants, comply with law, and explain an outcome.

Maintainers may correct or remove content, issue a warning, limit participation,
temporarily suspend access, or permanently ban a participant. The response should
be proportionate to severity, pattern, safety risk, and accountability. Knowingly
false or retaliatory reports may themselves violate this policy; an unsubstantiated
good-faith report does not.

Reports are handled on a best-effort basis without a fixed response deadline. When
practical, the reporter will receive an acknowledgement and a concise outcome.

This policy draws on the
[Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
