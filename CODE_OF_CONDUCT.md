# Community Code of Conduct

Field Runtime is building infrastructure for consequential work. The community
should model the clarity, accountability, and respect expected from that product.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and constructive.
- Critique ideas and implementations, not people.
- Make uncertainty and conflicts visible rather than hiding them.
- Respect privacy, attribution, and the boundaries of synthetic evaluation data.
- Help create a community where people of different backgrounds and experience
  levels can contribute safely.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized conduct, deliberate intimidation,
doxing, sustained disruption, and publication of another person's private
information without permission are not acceptable.

## Reporting and enforcement

Do not publish sensitive conduct reports in an issue. Use the repository's private
security-advisory channel and title the report `Community conduct report`:

<https://github.com/Mehtapozkan/fieldruntime-core/security/advisories/new>

Maintainers may remove content, restrict participation, or ban contributors whose
conduct is inconsistent with this policy. Reports will be handled as privately as
practical, subject to safety and legal obligations.

This policy is informed by the Contributor Covenant 2.1.
