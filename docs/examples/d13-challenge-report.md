# Synthetic invoice-dispute Challenge — worked account

This is the expected account from the [executable rehearsal](../guides/challenge-report.md#reproduce-the-synthetic-rehearsal),
not a customer comparison. Its generated HTML/JSON includes exact input hashes,
packets, citations, identity attribution and immutable receipts. All scope and
applicability statements are reconstructed from that export, never live permission.

**What work was demonstrated:** three records received a scoped checklist,
reconciliation and an unsent evidence request. There were seven invocation attempts
across three Cases and eight retained records. Four attempts produced preparation;
two packets were task-accepted for the same record. Repeats did not increase unique
coverage. The recurring population and previously unattended work are unknown.

Actual unsent request for Orchid challenge-1 includes:

> Please provide DEL-4 confirmation or identify the evidence owner. Please identify
> the governing terms and the person accountable for reviewing a disposition.

Its packet retains the limitation: missing confirmation does not establish
non-delivery or justify a credit, and no recipient or due date is assigned. The
exact draft and scoped CSV/note citations are expandable in the generated report.

**Proposed allocation:** retain deterministic scoped indexing/comparison/drafting;
a person establishes missing proof, governing terms, accountable ownership and task
usefulness. No model, adaptive agent, external tool or message was used. The report
allocates no financial consequence or closure permission.

Cash collected, disputes resolved, credits issued, work newly attended to and human
attention released remain **separate unknown aggregates**. Scripted synthetic notes
include a negative attention value of −5 person-minutes (10 baseline versus 15 actual),
an overlap reference, and a reopen/reversal of an unknown disposition note. These
illustrate retained evidence and corrections; they are not measured customer effort
or outcomes. Costs are separate and incomplete. Both preparation effort per accepted
packet and effort per verified business outcome remain unavailable.

Manual and generic-assistant comparisons have **not run**. Customer acceptance,
real impact, outcome cost and Case closure remain unproven. The ten-minute useful
packet, second-batch return and paid continuation targets remain experiments.
