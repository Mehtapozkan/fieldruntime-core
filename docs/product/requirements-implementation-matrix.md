  offer an approved upload-based evaluation; connected live shadow operation needs
  D17–D18 and production writes D20. Offline replay is not production operation.

  example split. No captured correction, automatic learning or promoted authority
  is implied by an approval or illustrative screen.

  ECC stays the only active pack and Orchid the only allowed simulated effect.
  Synthetic intake is merged; real samples still require
  the named data/access/retention/deletion amendment. No real-data approval is inferred here.
- **Foundation vocabulary versus implementation:** `OutcomeEvent` in the handoff
  maps to a proposed concept; the actual schema uses `Outcome`. WorkEvent occurrence,
  EvidenceRef retrieval and journal recording cannot silently stand in for ingestion.
  Schema-only correction, worker and metric concepts remain gaps.

  Design a bounded investigation task alongside it; implement after the result API,

  D15 still owns broad routing. A model is not a dependency of the synthetic result
  contract, and real customer preparation learning need not wait for model integration.
- **Reusable setup versus consent:** streamline presentation and identify reusable
  setup without silently reusing material consent, expanding scope or auto-starting
  work. Any such runtime change needs its own explicit decision; no new harness.
- **Platform-first versus early customer learning:** D9–D12 may run approved assisted
  evaluations alongside the sequence; do not wait for D14–D20 or fabricate economics
  to begin learning. No customer inputs enter the public synthetic appliance by default.
